Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 3, 2026

1. Introduction

Saaniya Software LLC ("Saaniya," "Company," "we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information that we collect and process.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, retain, and protect personal information when you visit our websites, create or use an account, use our software-as-a-service platforms, applications, educational and academy-management products, AI-enabled features, or other products and services, or otherwise interact with us (collectively, the "Services").

Saaniya is a for-profit limited liability company that provides software and technology services to businesses, academies, schools, organizations, and other customers and may also provide certain services directly to individual users. Where an organization provides access to our Services to its students, parents, personnel, customers, or other users, we may process personal information at the direction of that organization. In those circumstances, that organization may separately determine how and why your personal information is processed.

Our Services are operated from the United States. If you access our Services from outside the United States, please understand that your information may be transferred to, stored in, or processed in the United States and other countries in which we or our service providers operate.

2. Personal Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with us and which Services you use, we may collect the following categories of personal information:

Identifiers and Contact Information. Name, email address, mailing address, telephone number, username, account identifier, IP address, and similar identifiers.

Account and Profile Information. Account credentials, account settings, organization or academy affiliation, subscription information, preferences, and other information associated with your account.

Customer and Business Information. Information concerning a business, academy, school, organization, customer relationship, subscription, project, or other commercial relationship with Saaniya.

Transaction and Payment Information. Information regarding subscriptions, purchases, invoices, payment status, and transaction history. Payments may be processed by third-party payment processors. We do not intend to store complete payment-card numbers through our ordinary Services.

Product and Usage Information. Information concerning how you access and interact with our websites and Services, including pages or features used, actions taken, device and browser information, IP address, log information, and diagnostic and performance information.

Geolocation. We do not collect precise geolocation information for most of our products.

Precise Geolocation. Our fleet-operation SaaS product does not collect precise device location by default. If a customer enables an optional live-trip map and a driver starts a trip, approximate GPS from that driver device may be shared with the guest for that trip only.

Optional account connections. If a feature connects to email, calendar, or files, it connects to the user's own accounts, pursuant to the provider's consent screen. If you have questions concerning the privacy of your email or calendar accounts, you should consult the provider of those applications.

Google User Data / Limited Use. If you connect a Google account, we receive only data permitted by the scopes you approve. We use that data solely to provide the user-facing features you enable; we do not use it for advertising, credit decisions, sale to data brokers, or to train generalized or foundational AI models. Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Content You Provide. Information contained in forms, messages, support requests, files, records, documents, images, photos, feedback, or other information that you submit through the Services.

Academy, Student, and Parent Information. Certain academy or education-related Services may process information supplied by an academy, school, parent, or other customer, which may include student names, parent or guardian contact information, course or program information, enrollment information, attendance or progress information, and other information necessary to provide the applicable Service.

Artificial Intelligence Information. Certain Services may include artificial intelligence, machine learning, or automated features. If you interact with those features, we may process information that you submit to the feature, relevant account or product information, prompts, inputs, outputs, and information concerning your interaction with the feature.

Information from Third-Party Services. If you choose to connect your account to a third-party service, authentication provider, or other platform, we may receive information permitted by your settings and authorization with that provider.

AI Agents/ API Access. If you authorize your own AI agent to access the Services through our official API or per-user credentials you grant, we may receive information that agent sends on your behalf. You are responsible for what your agent sends; data it routes through its own service is handled under that service's terms.

We ask that you not submit sensitive personal information to the Services unless it is necessary for the particular Service and expressly permitted by us.

We and our service providers may use cookies, pixels, web beacons, software development kits, and similar technologies ("Tracking Technologies") in connection with our websites and Services.

Tracking Technologies may help us:

  • operate and secure our websites and Services;
  • authenticate users;

Some Tracking Technologies may collect information such as IP addresses, browser or device information, pages viewed, links selected, referring pages, approximate location derived from IP addresses, and timestamps associated with activity.

7. Global Privacy Control

Global Privacy Control ("GPC") is a browser-based signal through which users may communicate preferences regarding certain data-processing activities.

Some privacy laws require businesses meeting specified thresholds to recognize GPC or similar universal opt-out signals. Based on our current business operations, we may not be required to recognize such signals in every jurisdiction. We will evaluate our practices as our business and applicable legal requirements develop.

You may contact us using the information below regarding your privacy choices regardless of whether your browser transmits a GPC signal.
